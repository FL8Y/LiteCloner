# Third-party notices

This repository contains or rebuilds from the supplied EysClone prototype APK.

The prototype APK included notice/license files for third-party components. Those files are retained under `vendor/prototype-notices/assets/third-party-controls/`.

The new Shizuku bridge uses:

- Shizuku API/provider 13.1.5 — see the upstream project and its license.
- AndroidX annotations — see the dependency's license.

The original binary also contains VirtualApp, LibADB, BouncyCastle, SPAKE2 and other third-party material. Do not remove or overwrite the applicable notices when redistributing the rebuilt application.
